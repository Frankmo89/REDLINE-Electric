What Is an Electrical Service Call?

A service call is a diagnostic and repair visit for an existing electrical problem — an outlet or switch that's stopped working, a breaker that keeps tripping, flickering lights, a sparking outlet, exposed wiring, or a partial loss of power somewhere in the building. Unlike a planned project, service calls usually start with "something's wrong" rather than a scoped-out job.

We also handle service calls tied to home inspections — if an inspection report flags electrical issues during a home sale, we can assess and repair what's listed so it's resolved before closing. For anything that looks or feels like an active hazard — sparking, burning smells, exposed wiring — we treat it as an emergency call.

What's Included

  • On-site diagnosis to find the actual cause of the problem
  • Repair or replacement of the faulty wiring, device, or breaker
  • Code-compliant fixes, including GFCI and AFCI protection where required
  • Repairs tied to home inspection reports ahead of a sale
  • Emergency response for hazards like sparking outlets or exposed wiring

When You Need This

Call for a service visit any time something electrical stops working correctly, feels warm or smells off, or a breaker won't stay reset — these are usually signs of an underlying issue rather than something to wait out. For anything that looks actively dangerous, treat it as an emergency and call right away.

What to Expect

When you call, tell us what you're seeing — that helps us show up prepared for the right repair. We diagnose the issue on-site, explain what's causing it, and quote the repair before we do the work. If the fix turns out to be bigger than expected, like a panel issue, we'll walk you through the options before moving forward.
